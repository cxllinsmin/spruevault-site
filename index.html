BUILT FOR MODEL KIT BUILDERS

More than a list of kits.

SprueVault is a personal hobby database for the whole workflow — from the kits you want, to the builds you finish, to the tools, paints, materials, photos, and stats behind them.

01 / VAULT

Your entire collection, organized your way.

Switch between multiple Vault layouts, manually reorder kits, and narrow large collections with a dedicated filter sidebar for status, grade, scale, manufacturer, series, and more.

Multiple views Sidebar filters Manual ordering Status tracking

02 / WISHLIST

Plan what comes next.

Keep future purchases organized with priorities, desired prices, preferred stores, manual ordering, and a running wishlist total.

Priority levels Target prices Store tracking Move to collection

03 / BUILD GALLERY

Give every build a visual history.

Keep build photos together, filter them by stage, kit, grade, or series, and manage your gallery without opening every image one at a time.

Build stages Sidebar filters Bulk photo tools Save to Photos

04 / KIT DETAILS

A project file for every kit.

Keep ratings, kit details, build progress, photography, sessions, build time, notes, paint usage, and gallery history together.

Ratings Build progress Session logs Build time

05 / BUILD SESSIONS

Track time at the workbench.

Record build sessions, keep session photos connected to your projects, and see exactly where your hobby time is going.

Build timer Session logs Build stages Session photos

Keep your whole workbench in one place.
