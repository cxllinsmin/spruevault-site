More than a list of kits.

SprueVault is a personal workshop database for the whole hobby — from the kits you want, to the builds you finish, to the paint sitting on your shelf.

01 / COLLECTION

See your whole collection at a glance.

Track backlog, building, completed, and sold kits with custom ordering, multiple views, favorites, photos, grades, scales, manufacturers, purchase details, and more.
