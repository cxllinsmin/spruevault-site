PRIVACY POLICY

Your hobby data stays yours.

Effective: September 14, 2026

Overview

SprueVault is a model kit collection and build tracker. The app is designed to keep your collection information, wishlist, build records, photos, paint inventory, and related hobby data on your device.

Data Collection

SprueVault does not require an account and does not operate an advertising or analytics service that collects your personal information.

Photos and Camera Access

If you choose to add images, SprueVault may request access to your camera or photo library. Access is used only so you can select or capture images for your kits and builds.

App Data

Collection data, wishlist information, build sessions, build photos, paint inventory, preferences, and other records created in SprueVault are stored locally on your device.

Backup and Restore

SprueVault includes manual backup and restore features. Backup files are created only when you choose to export them. You control where those files are stored or shared.

Third-Party Services

SprueVault does not sell your personal data and does not use your hobby data for advertising. Standard Apple platform services may process technical information as part of normal iOS and App Store operation, subject to Apple's own policies.

Children

SprueVault is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as SprueVault changes. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to collins.minyangadou@outlook.com.
